# Project Brief: Project Vitality
**SVC Student Health Blueprint**  
*A Delhi University Chapters Initiative*  
*Source: https://manandewan.github.io/Project-Vitality/*

---

## 1. Overview & Core Mission

* **Initiative**: Project Vitality (SV College Chapter, Delhi University).
* **Tagline**: *Cultivating a Health-Conscious Campus*.
* **Executive Summary**: A 100% zero-budget, student-operated blueprint designed to improve actual physiological biomarkers (cortisol, glucose, and heart rate) at Sri Venkateswara College (SVC).
* **Core Problem**: College students neglect physical health due to irregular routines, poor food availability, and an absence of biomarker tracking.
* **Mission**: Design and implement a sustainable, student-led health initiative that improves actual student biomarkers (lipid profiles, blood sugar, stress metrics), shifting away from superficial "gym-bro" physical aesthetics.
* **Methodology**: Combines behavioral science, seasonal mapping, digital audits, and local partnerships to institutionalize wellness as a standard component of Delhi University campus life.

---

## 2. Realities & Constraints

The initiative operates as a student-led organization with zero institutional funding, meaning all projects are filtered through three strict feasibility constraints:

1. **Zero Budget**:
   * All operations rely on zero cash outlay.
   * Rewards and prizes are sourced as coupons and promotions from local cafes and printing services.
2. **Limited Volunteer Hours**:
   * Volunteers are full-time students managing lectures, assignments, and exams.
   * All initiatives must require minimal ongoing maintenance time.
3. **Cultural Resistance**:
   * Traditional wellness is commonly viewed by students as "uncool" or preachy.
   * Campaigns must actively utilize gamification, humor, and digital-first delivery to build engagement.

## 4. Campus Wellness Initiatives Breakdown

### Pillar 1: Awareness
* **Core Essence**: Low-barrier cognitive framing. Shifting wellness from a preachy chore to a performance-oriented "biohack" that requires nothing but passive attention.
* **Objective**: Capturing attention, educating, and altering student mindsets without demanding active behavioral changes.
* **Initiatives**:
  * **1.1 Digital Canteen Guide**: Crowdsourced mobile food metrics and high-protein recommendations.
  * **1.2 Mythbusters Series**: Meme-based carousels debunking campus wellness myths.
  * **1.3 Vitality Micro-Broadcasts**: 15-minute Instagram/Zoom live Q&As with health experts.
  * **1.4 Dorm Room Hacks Videos**: Fast-paced mobile Reels detailing cheap circadian and sleep hacks.
  * **1.5 Nutrition Decoders**: Translating packaged snack sugars and sodium into visual equivalents.
  * **1.6 Seminars with Industry Experts**: Live physical expert talks coordinated directly during class times.

---

### Pillar 2: Impact
* **Core Essence**: Active behavioral conversion. Driving direct updates to student biomarkers by negotiating institutional food swaps or organizing gamified challenges.
* **Objective**: Implementing structured, actionable interventions on campus that demand active student participation or physical environment upgrades.
* **Initiatives**:
  * **2.1 Canteen Condition Analysis**: Auditing current canteen prep and offering zero-cost menu upgrades backed by student petitions.
  * **2.2 On-Campus Biomarker Health Camp**: Low-cost biomarker testing camp operated entirely by external lab clinical staff.
  * **2.3 VenkyWalks Step Challenge**: Departmental walking leagues during winter with AQI-safety fallback.
  * **2.4 Impromptu Challenges**: Live push-up/pull-up break activations with immediate sponsored tokens.

---

### Pillar 3: Research
* **Core Essence**: Empirical proof-of-concept. Gathering localized metrics to build a database, providing the leverage to lobby administrators.
* **Objective**: Conducting academic and analytical study frameworks throughout the year to understand the college health baseline and revise policies.
* **Initiatives**:
  * **3.1 Annual Health Census**: Anonymous 3-minute Google Form mapping student sleep, diet, and fatigue.
  * **3.2 PG Housing Diet Audits**: Mobile diet diaries surveying off-campus tiffin provider deficits.
  * **3.3 Reference-Based Food Estimates**: USDA/IFCT recipe audits mapping canteen staples.

* **Target Biomarkers**: Cortisol, glucose, heart rate, lipid profiles, blood sugar, and stress metrics.
