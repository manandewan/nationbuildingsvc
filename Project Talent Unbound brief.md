# Project Brief: Talent Unbound
**A NationBuilding Impact Chapters Initiative**  
*Ecosystem Partner: Sri Venkateswara College (SVC), University of Delhi*

---

## 1. Executive Summary

**Project Talent Unbound** is an initiative spearheaded by **NationBuilding Impact Chapters** to systematically dismantle employment barriers faced by college students and alumni with physical, sensory, and neurocognitive disabilities (Persons with Benchmark Disabilities - PwBD).

Piloted within **Sri Venkateswara College (SVC)** over an 8-month timeframe, the project designs and institutionalizes an end-to-end pathway comprising campus-based placement readiness, accessible recruitment channels, and sensitized employer partnerships. The broader mandate is to create a scalable, pan-university model aligned with India’s **Rights of Persons with Disabilities (RPwD) Act, 2016** and the national vision of equitable economic empowerment.

---

## 2. Problem Statement & Context

### The Guiding Question
> *"How can we design and implement effective pathways within Sri Venkateswara College over the next eight months to create meaningful skilled work opportunities for students and alumni with varied physical, sensory, and neurocognitive disabilities?"*

### Problem Breakdown
* **The Core Problem**: PwBD students face systemic exclusion in mainstream campus recruitment—manifested in inaccessible assessment drives, low recruiter awareness, and an absence of tailored career readiness support.
* **Current Situation**:
  * Persistent lack of inclusivity within college placement cell operations.
  * Minimal proactive corporate outreach targeting skilled roles for this student segment.
* **Key Complications**:
  * Limited engagement between placement cells and students with disabilities.
  * Misconceptions and unconscious biases regarding student capabilities.
  * Standardized recruitment tests, platforms, and interview protocols remain unadapted to sensory, motor, or neurocognitive needs.
* **Constraints within the Solution Space**:
  * Hesitation and limited initial participation from corporate recruiters.
  * Shortage of dedicated career counsellors and specialized mentors.
  * Logistical, operational, and infrastructural constraints in executing tailored placement assessments.
  * Resource limitations hindering outreach and customized program delivery.

---

## 3. Strategic Vision & Core Objectives

### Vision Statement
To build an inclusive employment model within the SVC ecosystem that empowers students with disabilities to achieve professional success through campus-based placement readiness, accessible recruitment channels, and sensitized employer partnerships.

### Core Objectives Matrix

| Pillar | Strategic Goal | Key Expected Outcome |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **1. Enable Access** | Remove logistical & procedural hurdles | Inclusive placement systems & adaptive recruitment pipelines |
| **2. Build Capability** | Prepare candidates for skilled roles | Targeted career training, mentorship pairings & skill development |
| **3. Drive Sensitization** | De-stigmatize disability in hiring | Comprehensive awareness for recruiters, faculty & student bodies |
| **4. Facilitate Linkages** | Connect talent with real opportunities | Direct bridges between PwBD candidates & vetted, inclusive employers |
| **5. Institutionalize Inclusion** | Ensure permanence & sustainability | An institutionalized annual inclusive placement framework embedded in college policy |

---

## 4. Scope & Boundaries

* **In-Scope**:
  * Developing a sustainable, closed-loop system integrated directly within the college ecosystem.
  * Leveraging campus-native programs (e.g., **SRIVIPRA** – a faculty-led research internship program).
  * Crafting employability curriculum modules, mentorship programs, and an **Inclusive Recruitment Toolkit** for employers.
  * Formulation of policy recommendations and structural governance within the placement committee.
* **Out-of-Scope**:
  * Guaranteeing commercial employment outcomes or unilateral job offer mandates.
  * University-wide statutory overhauls or state/central legislative reforms.

## 6. Implementation Framework (4-Phase Roadmap)

### Phase 1: Discovery & Baseline (Months 1–2)
* **Key Actions**:
  * Conduct semi-structured qualitative interviews with **30–50 PwBD students** to uncover specific barriers, aspirations, and accessibility needs.
  * Map the current placement ecosystem and quantify gaps (participation rates, assessment accessibility, support infrastructure).
  * Multi-stakeholder consultations across students, faculty, EOC, administration, and corporate recruiters.
* **Key Deliverables**: *Baseline Gap Report*, *Stakeholder Map*, *Metrics Dashboard*.

### Phase 2: Strategy Design (Months 3–5)
* **Key Actions**:
  * **Capability**: Build a tailored employability curriculum, 1-on-1 industry mentorship pairings, and adaptive skill-learning modules.
  * **Partnerships**: Segment recruiters into *Champions*, *Willing*, and *Skeptics*; develop corporate sensitization training and an **Inclusive Recruitment Toolkit**.
  * **Institutionalization**: Formulate accessible placement protocols, institutional policy recommendations, and governance structures.
* **Key Deliverables**: *Operating Model*, *Partnership Playbook*, *Adaptive Training Modules*.

### Phase 3: Pilot & Validate (Months 6–7)
* **Key Actions**:
  * Launch a targeted pilot with an initial cohort of **5–6 students** and **5–8 inclusion-ready corporate recruiters**.
  * Conduct interactive sensitization workshops for recruiters, faculty members, and placement coordinators.
  * Execute tailored mock placement drives and track end-to-end recruitment KPIs.
* **Key Deliverables**: *Proof of Concept (PoC)*, *Impact Report*, *Validated Case Studies & Success Stories*.

### Phase 4: Scale & Institutionalize (Month 8 and Ongoing)
* **Key Actions**:
  * Expand the tested framework to the entire PwBD student population across all departments.
  * Formalize dedicated inclusive recruitment drives directly into the official annual SVC placement calendar.
* **Key Deliverables**: *Sustainable Operating Framework*, *Institutionalized Annual Placement System*.

## 7. Sources of Insight & Research Methodology

* **Primary Research**: First-hand interviews with PwBD students and alumni, and direct engagement with the college's Equal Opportunity Cell (EOC).
* **Secondary Research**: Industry disability employment benchmarks, research reports, and statutory frameworks (notably the **Rights of Persons with Disabilities Act, 2016** and relevant government welfare schemes).

---

## 8. Success Criteria & Metrics

### Qualitative Metrics
* Enhanced student self-efficacy, employability, and interview confidence through targeted workshops.
* Measurable reduction in employer bias and workplace stigma surrounding disability recruitment.
* Institutional recognition of Sri Venkateswara College as an inclusion-first campus.

### Quantitative Metrics
* **Opportunity Expansion**: Increased volume of accessible job drives and tailored placement processes.
* **Conversion Rate**: Higher transition of internships or temporary roles into full-time, paid employment.
* **Retention Rate**: Improved 6- to 12-month retention rates within hiring organizations.

---

## 9. Beneficiary Impact

* **Students with Disabilities**: Acquisition of high-demand skills, professional confidence, self-reliance, and sustainable career trajectories.
* **Employers**: Direct pipeline to diverse, skilled, and resilient talent driving innovation, perspective, and genuine DEI culture within teams.
* **Community & Institution**: Enhanced social mobility for individuals and families, setting a benchmark for Delhi University and higher education institutions at large.

---

## 10. Long-Term Vision

**Project Talent Unbound** is designed as a replicable blueprint. Following successful institutionalization at Sri Venkateswara College, the initiative aims to expand across Delhi University colleges, establishing a pan-university inclusive employment network in lockstep with the **RPwD Act, 2016** and **NationBuilding's** overarching mission for equitable nation-wide opportunities.
