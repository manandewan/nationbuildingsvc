The National Network

How this chapter differs from other student bodies

An ecosystem where leadership is earned, impact is measured, and purpose meets possibility.

01

Bottom-Up Ownership

Students don't just execute top-down mandates—they discover campus problems, vote on priorities, and author the solutions.

02

Structured Leadership Pathways

Every position is earned through an open and competitive application process, building real-world organizational readiness.

03

Real-Time Mentorship

Work directly with domain experts, policymakers, college administration, and the central BuildingIndia team.

04

PPI Opportunities & Recognition

Pre-Placement Interview (PPI) opportunities with Nation with NaMo for standout leaders, verified LORs, and tiered campus recognition.

05

Hyperlocal Action to Real Impact

Translate analytical models into tangible change by mobilizing students and stakeholders directly within your college community.

06

Shared National Community

Be part of a growing pan-India network with regular city meetups across IITs, BITS Pilani, and Delhi University colleges.

Governance Architecture

Chapter structure & recruitment standards

Tenure commences on 1st August and concludes on 30th July, constituting one full academic year of institutional leadership.

Team Hierarchy

Organized into three complementary bodies ensuring strategic continuity and ground-level execution.

Executive Body
President (Final Year) & Vice Presidents (Standard, College, Marketing Initiatives)
Management Body
Chapter Coordinators (Pre-Final Year Students) leading project verticals
General Body
Associates (First & Pre-Final Year Students) executing ground research & events

3-Stage Selection Process

Every position is earned through an open and competitive merit-based application workflow.

1

Application Form

Detailed evaluation of problem-solving curiosity, commitment, and alignment with nation-building values.

2

Group Discussion (GD)

Collaborative problem-solving evaluation conducted by the Chapter Management Body.

3

Personal Interview

Comprehensive discussion on project execution ownership, time management, and ethical leadership.
